Sleep Buddy: Soundscapes

Build a soundscape that feels right for bedtime. Mix white noise with rain, ocean waves, flowing water, wildlife, trains, and more. Adjust each sound, save your favourite mixes, and use a sleep timer to wind down.

Features

  • 12 curated soundscapes, from Rain Season to Train Journey
  • More than 50 sounds: white noise, rain, ocean, rivers, wildlife, trains and more, all generated on your device
  • Play several sounds at once and adjust or mute each one, or the whole group
  • Save, rename and reorder your own mixes
  • Sleep timer with a gentle fade-out, and a daily wake-up alarm with 10 soft tones
  • Works offline, with a calm night look (dark by default) and a light option
  • Works on iPhone and iPad
  • Available in English

Free and Premium

Sleep Buddy is free to download. The free plan includes 6 soundscapes and 28 sounds, with ads. Watch two short ads to unlock any other soundscape or sound for 24 hours (up to 2 soundscapes and 3 sounds a day).

Premium unlocks every soundscape and sound with no ads, as a one-time Lifetime purchase (USD $9.99) or a monthly subscription (USD $2.99). Prices may differ in your region and are shown in the app. Cancel the monthly subscription anytime in your Apple Account settings.

This app provides ambient sounds for relaxation and sleep routines. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or cure sleep disorders. Results vary, and the app cannot guarantee that you will fall asleep. If sleep problems persist, affect your daily life, or concern you, speak with a qualified health professional.
