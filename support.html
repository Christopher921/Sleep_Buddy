Support

Need help with Sleep Buddy: Soundscapes? Email us at chowchowbuy@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

What's free, and what does Premium add?

The free plan includes 6 soundscapes and 28 sounds, with ads. To try anything else, watch two short ads to unlock that soundscape or sound for 24 hours (up to 2 soundscapes and 3 sounds a day). Premium unlocks everything with no ads, either as a one-time Lifetime purchase or a monthly subscription.

How do I cancel the monthly subscription?

Open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Sleep Buddy: Soundscapes. You can also tap Manage Subscription in the app's Settings. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

I bought Premium on another device. How do I get access?

Sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Restore Purchases in the app's Settings or on the Premium screen. This works for both Lifetime and Monthly.

Why did the sound stop?

Sounds stop when the sleep timer ends, when you unplug headphones, or during phone calls. Sounds keep playing with the screen locked. Make sure your device volume is up and silent mode or Focus settings aren't affecting playback.

How do I play several sounds together?

In the Sounds tab, tap as many sounds as you like and they start playing right away. The sounds playing appear at the top of the screen, where you can remove one or change the volume of the whole group. Use the save button there, or Add to My Mix, to keep the combination in My Mixes.

How does the wake-up alarm work?

Turn it on in Settings > Alarm, pick a time and one of 10 soft tones. It repeats every day at that time until you turn it off. The alarm is delivered as a notification, so it works even if the app is closed.

Does the alarm sound in Silent mode or Do Not Disturb?

Not always. Because the alarm is a notification, iOS controls it: in Silent mode it vibrates instead of playing its tone (depending on your Sounds & Haptics settings), and a Focus such as Do Not Disturb or Sleep can silence it unless you allow Sleep Buddy: Soundscapes in that Focus. If Sleep Buddy is still open or playing sounds at alarm time, the tone plays in the app at the alarm volume you chose. For important wake-ups, also use the Clock app.

Why didn't my alarm go off?

It can't sound if notifications for Sleep Buddy: Soundscapes are turned off, the device is off, or system settings such as Focus block it. Check iOS Settings > Notifications > Sleep Buddy: Soundscapes.

How do I change my ad and tracking choices?

Ads appear only on the free plan; Premium removes them. To change whether apps can track you, go to iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you're in a region where ad consent is required, tap Ad Privacy Choices in the app's Settings to review your choice.

What happens to my saved mixes if my subscription ends?

Your mixes stay on your device. On the free plan, Premium sounds in a mix are left out until you unlock them or go Premium again. Deleting the app deletes your mixes.

How do I turn off usage stats?

Turn off Share anonymous usage stats in the app's Settings. See the Privacy Policy for what is collected.

Do the sounds work offline?

Yes. Every sound is generated on your device, so no internet connection is needed to play them.
