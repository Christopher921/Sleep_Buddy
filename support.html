How does the free trial work?

The 7-day free trial starts when you confirm it in the app. You get full access during the trial. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, it becomes a monthly subscription charged to your Apple Account.

How do I cancel?

Open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Sleep Buddy: Soundscapes. You can also tap Manage Subscription in the app's Settings. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

I subscribed on another device. How do I get access?

Sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Restore Purchases in the app's Settings or on the subscription screen.

Why did the sound stop?

Sounds stop when the sleep timer ends, when you unplug headphones, or during phone calls. Sounds keep playing with the screen locked. Make sure your device volume is up and silent mode or Focus settings aren't affecting playback.

Why didn't my alarm go off?

The alarm is delivered as a notification. It can't sound if notifications for Sleep Buddy: Soundscapes are turned off, the device is off, or system settings such as Focus block it. Check iOS Settings > Notifications > Sleep Buddy: Soundscapes.

What happens to my saved mixes if my subscription ends?

Your mixes stay on your device but can't be opened until you subscribe again. Deleting the app deletes them.

Do the sounds work offline?

Yes. Every sound is generated on your device, so no internet connection is needed to play them.
